The Thread

The convergence of AI harm and AI infrastructure dominates today's signals. While markets celebrate record highs driven by memory chip euphoria, the Radnor High School deepfake case exposes AI's devastating potential when weaponized against children. This isn't just another tech story — it's a preview of social fractures that AI tools will create as they become more accessible and sophisticated. The infrastructure buildout continues at breakneck speed, but we're seeing the first serious reckoning with AI's darker applications. The Memory ETF's historic growth trajectory reflects Wall Street's bet on AI's massive computational demands, while GitHub's co-founder rebuilds version control specifically for AI agents. These aren't separate trends — they're part of the same transformation that's reshaping how we build, deploy, and control intelligent systems.
