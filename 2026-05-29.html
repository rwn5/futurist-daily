Commentary & Synthesis

The Thread

The enshittification of AI is accelerating. Character.AI's descent into ad-cluttered mediocrity represents the first major case study of how AI platforms degrade once they capture users. Meanwhile, Headway's biometric demands show how healthcare AI leverages desperation—forcing vulnerable patients to surrender facial scans or lose treatment access. The real story isn't just platform decay but the emergence of AI as critical infrastructure. When mental health care becomes conditional on facial scanning and financial analysis shifts to AI CFOs, we're not just adopting tools—we're rewiring essential services around algorithmic gatekeepers.
"Models being conscious would be harmful for humanity. It would encroach on our status and dignity."
Anthropic's $47 billion revenue run-rate reveals the economics driving this transformation. The money flowing into AI isn't just funding research—it's creating dependencies that make retreat impossible. Cities covering surveillance cameras with trash bags while unable to cancel contracts perfectly captures our moment: we're building systems we can't control or escape, then scrambling to hide from our own creations. The cognitive dissonance between AI promise and AI reality is becoming a defining tension of the decade.
