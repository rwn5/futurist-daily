Microsoft and OpenAI have formally acknowledged that AI crawlers are degrading the web — while simultaneously flooding Spotify with AI-generated slop under real artists' names. The admission lands as AI agent spam officially becomes a mainstream problem, not a fringe concern.

Coding Agents Make Software Engineering Harder, Not Easier

Willison's blunt take: coding agents are powerful but raise the skill floor, not lower it. Unlocking their full potential demands extraordinary discipline. That's the opposite of the "anyone can code now" narrative.

Meta Muse Opens to Developers — Is This the AI App Store Moment?

Meta has opened its Muse personal AI agent to third-party connectors, letting developers plug their services directly into user conversations. Isenberg calls it the App Store moment for AI — and the comparison is hard to dismiss given each user runs their own persistent Linux VM in Meta's cloud.

Commentary & Synthesis

The Thread

The internet is now eating itself, and the companies responsible are starting to say so out loud. OpenAI admitting its crawlers harm the web while simultaneously profiting from the content those crawlers scraped is a remarkable moment of institutional honesty — or damage control. Either way, it signals we've crossed into a phase where AI's externalities are too visible to deny.
Coding agents make software engineering harder. The tools got more powerful; the job got more demanding. That's not a contradiction — it's the pattern.
Meta opening Muse to developers is the story everyone should be watching more carefully. A persistent Linux VM per user, combined with a connector ecosystem, is infrastructure for an entirely new app economy — one where the interface is conversation and the App Store is a prompt. If that surfaces legitimate network effects, every other platform's agent strategy looks reactive by comparison. The self-improving AI story from a16z and Mirendil is worth filing for later. The founders built the research foundations at Google and Anthropic — they're not speculating. When people with that résumé say "AI contributing to its own development is the next layer," the timeline question gets uncomfortable fast. Meanwhile markets barely flinched: VIX at 15, Bitcoin holding above $84K. The world continues pricing in stability that the underlying technology is quietly undermining.
