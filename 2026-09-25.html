AI & Technology

404 MEDIA

FBI Hack Exposed the FBI's Own Hacking Unit

The FBI's Remote Operations Unit — a classified team that builds exploits and intrusion tools — had members exposed in a breach linked to ShinyHunters. The agency that hacks others just got hacked back, and the fallout for ongoing operations could be significant.

ANTHROPIC / THE NEURON DAILY

950 Claude Agents Uncover a New Enzyme System

Anthropic deployed a swarm of 950 Claude agents that discovered a molecular machine potentially representing a new gene editing mechanism. Dario Amodei confirmed the finding, calling it a genuine scientific discovery rather than a parlor trick. This is the clearest signal yet that AI is doing real wet-lab-adjacent science.

GOOGLE DEEPMIND

Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS Arrives With 2,000+ Voices and Promptable Design

Google released gemini-3.8-flash-tts and a lite variant, featuring over 2,000 stock voices and a new voice design system you can prompt directly. Simon Willison already has a live playground up. The audio AI arms race is accelerating fast.

MIRENDIL / A16Z

The Case for AI That Improves Itself

Former Google and Anthropic researchers launched Mirendil around one question: what happens when AI meaningfully contributes to its own development? The self-accelerating AI thesis is moving from thought experiment to funded startup. Watch this space.

COINDESK

Federal Reserve Moves to Implement GENIUS Act for Stablecoins

The Fed is formally advancing proposals to operationalize the GENIUS Act stablecoin framework. Regulatory infrastructure for digital dollars is being built in real time, even as New York sues Polymarket for illegal gambling and Bitget absorbs a $352 million hack via spoofed transfers.

404 MEDIA

Earth's Magnetic Field Has a Mysterious Effect on Aging

Scientists found that diseased fruit flies lived longer when removed from Earth's magnetic field, while healthy flies died sooner — a first-of-its-kind result nobody expected. The implications for human longevity research are wide open and genuinely strange.

03
Commentary & Synthesis

The Thread

Two threads are colliding this week that deserve to be read together. First: Anthropic's 950-agent swarm found a potentially novel gene editing mechanism. Second: Mirendil — founded by ex-Google and Anthropic researchers — is explicitly building AI that improves itself. These aren't separate stories. They're sequential chapters. The question is no longer whether AI can do science. It's how quickly the loop closes between AI doing science and AI doing

AI
science.

"The thing that actually matters in AI development right now is just the rate of capability improvement." — Ethan Mollick The security picture is deteriorating on every front simultaneously. The FBI's own hacking unit got exposed. Bitget lost $352 million to spoofed transfers. A16z spent the week publishing interviews about how to secure agentic systems — not a coincidence. As agents gain real permissions, real wallets, and real access, the attack surface isn't growing linearly. It's compounding. The same week AI agents are discovering enzymes, they're also becoming the most attractive target in any system they touch. Meanwhile the macro environment is applying pressure from below. Mortgage rates at 7.45%, bond yields spiking 30 bps in two days, crypto retreating — Bitcoin off nearly 3%, altcoins getting hit harder. The Federal Reserve advancing stablecoin implementation rules while New York sues Polymarket captures the regulatory contradiction perfectly: the infrastructure is being formalized at the same moment the enforcement walls are going up. The next six months will clarify which force wins.
