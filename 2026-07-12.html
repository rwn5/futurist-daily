The new GPT-5.6 family: Luna, Terra, Sol

OpenAI's latest flagship hits general availability in three sizes — Luna, Terra, and Sol — with pricing that undercuts both Claude Opus and Claude Fable 5 at comparable performance tiers. Patreon Blocks Crawlers From Stealing Creators' Work for AI Training

Patreon has partnered with Cloudflare to block AI training crawlers, with CEO Jack Conte drawing a hard line on consent and compensation. It's one of the more aggressive platform-level responses to unauthorized scraping. Expect others to follow.

AI Fiction Is Easy to Detect Because It's Stupid and Bad, Research Finds

New research finds AI-generated fiction has distinct tells: ChatGPT leans on dream sequences, Gemini over-describes characters. Detection is less about watermarks and more about patterns of aesthetic failure. The models are consistent in their mediocrity.

Introducing Muse Spark 1.1

Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 arrives with the first public API for the Spark model family, claiming significant improvements in agentic tool calling and computer use. It enters a crowded field but Meta's distribution advantage shouldn't be dismissed. The API opening is the real story here.

The FTC's new settlement with John Deere has actual enforcement teeth, unlike prior agreements that changed little. Farmers can now repair their own equipment without voiding warranties or being locked out by proprietary software. A quiet but significant win for hardware ownership rights.

Commentary & Synthesis

The Thread

The model wars just got a pricing floor. GPT-5.6 Sol at $5/$30 per million tokens is OpenAI playing offense against Anthropic, and the timing — alongside Grok 4.5 landing at what benchmarkers are calling the Pareto frontier — means we're in a genuine three-way shootout. Builders are running live comparisons in public, and the consensus is forming fast: cost curves are collapsing faster than anyone expected six months ago.

The real competition isn't capability anymore. It's who owns the developer's default.
The more interesting pressure is coming from the platform layer. Patreon blocking crawlers, Google offering free deployment URLs, Meta opening the Muse Spark API — these aren't isolated product moves. They're territory grabs. Every platform is either fortifying its data moat or racing to become the place where the next generation of AI-native apps gets built and distributed.

Meanwhile the Hormuz situation is a stress test the market is choosing to ignore. Crypto flat, VIX down, equities up — on a day the US military conducted strikes in a global oil chokepoint. Either this de-escalates fast or the complacency reads as a setup. Worth watching Monday open closely. The rotation from crypto to AI capital continues either way; geopolitics just adds a variable no model is pricing cleanly.
