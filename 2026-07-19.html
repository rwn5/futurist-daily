Commentary & Synthesis

The Thread

The through-line this week is institutional AI running ahead of institutional wisdom. The Nik Suresh piece — surfaced by Willison — captures something measurable: companies are restructuring around AI with the same energy they once brought to Six Sigma or blockchain, and with similar rates of magical thinking. Boardrooms confusing capability demonstrations for deployment roadmaps is not new. What's new is the speed of the damage.
"AI mania is not about AI. It's about the oldest human failure mode: substituting enthusiasm for analysis."
Meanwhile the model arms race continues indifferent to any of this. Kimi K3 at 2.8 trillion parameters, Claude Fable 5 going permanent on premium tiers, Bun rewriting itself in Rust to shave Claude Code's startup time. The infrastructure layer is compounding quietly while the strategy layer burns. France blocking Polymarket and ICE spending $125M on Thomson Reuters data are two governments doing what governments do when they feel control slipping — reaching for the tools at hand, however blunt. The market signal is worth noting: equities down, VIX up 12%, Bitcoin holding above $63K and climbing. That divergence is not noise. Whether it reflects genuine rotation or just crypto's usual disconnection from macro stress, the Kobeissi labor force number — 832K Americans exiting in a single month — sits underneath all of it as an unresolved weight.
