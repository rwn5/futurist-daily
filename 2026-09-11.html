SIMON WILLISON / CALIF RESEARCH

Zero-Click WeChat Worm Spreads Through Calls — No Answer Required

Researchers at Calif.io released a demo of WeWorm, the first zero-click worm to propagate via WeChat calls on both iOS and Android. The victim doesn't need to answer — or even touch their phone. The team found the underlying bug using AI-assisted research, which is the part that should keep security teams awake.

Mirendil Wants to Build AI That Improves Itself

Former Google and Anthropic researchers launched Mirendil around one question: what happens when AI meaningfully contributes to its own development? This is the self-accelerating AI thesis made explicit as a company, not just a research paper.

COINDESK

India Tokenizes $620B Corporate Bond Market With Digital Rupee

India is moving its corporate bond market onto tokenized rails settled in digital rupees. At $620 billion, this is the largest real-world asset tokenization deployment by a sovereign economy to date — not a pilot, an actual market structure shift.

Mullenweg Put on Leave at Automattic After Board Move

WordPress co-founder Matt Mullenweg told staff via Slack that board members "conspired" to vote him onto a leave of absence. The open-source web's most powerful executive is now sidelined, and the governance crisis at Automattic just escalated sharply.

Cybersecurity Assumptions Are Breaking Under Agentic AI

A16z's security deep-dive series makes the case that traditional defenses were built to stop humans and known malware — not frontier models that can autonomously find and chain vulnerabilities. Identity, permissions, and monitoring all need to be rebuilt from scratch for the agentic era.
